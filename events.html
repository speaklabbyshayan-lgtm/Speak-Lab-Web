SpeakLab Events

Communication Walks, Open Mics and One-Day Workshops — every week in Lahore, open to everyone

Every week · Lahore

Practice that happens outside the classroom

A course teaches you the language. These events are where you actually use it — in front of strangers, on a stage, on your feet. Pick the one that scares you slightly.

📍 Held in Lahore 🔁 Every week 🙌 Open to everyone 💬 English-only environment
🚶

Communication Walks

Real conversations, on the move.

A guided walk through the city where the only rule is: speak English. You are paired with a new partner every few minutes and given a real topic to talk about. No desks, no marks, no waiting for your turn — just conversation until it stops feeling difficult.

  • FormatOutdoor walk, rotating partners, coach on hand
  • LengthAbout 90 minutes
  • Best forAnyone who knows English but freezes when speaking it
Register for a Walk
🎤

Open Mics

Five minutes. One mic. Your voice.

An evening where anyone can take the stage — a story, an opinion, a poem, a pitch, or something you have never said out loud before. A warm room that claps first and critiques kindly, plus direct feedback from the SpeakLab team when you want it.

  • FormatLive stage, 5 minutes each, optional feedback
  • LengthAbout 2 hours
  • Best forStage fear, interview nerves, anyone who wants a mic
Register for an Open Mic
🎯

One-Day Workshop

One focused day, one skill sharpened.

A full intensive built around a single theme — interviews, presentations, storytelling or debate. Short teaching, long practice: you spend most of the day speaking, being recorded, and being corrected. You leave with one skill measurably better than you arrived with.

  • FormatFull-day intensive, drills and live practice
  • LengthOne full day
  • Best forProfessionals and students who want a fast, focused reset
Register for a Workshop

You don't have to be a SpeakLab student

These events are open to the city. If you are enrolled with us, they are part of how you practise between classes. If you are not, you are just as welcome — plenty of people come to the events first and decide about the program later, or never join at all and keep coming anyway.

🎓 Enrolled SpeakLab students

Your classroom practice, applied in the real world. Come as often as you like — this is where the 8 weeks actually stick.

👋 Everyone else

No enrollment, no test, no commitment. Register, show up, speak. Students, professionals, and anyone who wants to get better at being heard.
